Organisation

Transportation

We recommend traveling from Porto to Chaves by car. You can rent one in advance at the airport. From the main exit, rental agencies are located in two places: 1) Directly on your right, follow the bus spaces and cross the pedestrian crossings.
View on Google Maps 2) Take the shuttle to the north part of the airport. It runs every 5-10 minutes.
View on Google Maps

Accommodations

Casas Novas Hotel, where the cocktail and meals will take place, is located 15 minutes from the city center of Chaves.
www.hotelcasasnovas.com
All rooms at this hotel have been pre-reserved for our families and friends with children as a priority. From September 25, 2027, if there are still rooms available, they can be reserved by other guests. It is also possible to book a room at another nearby hotel, A Casa do Meio do Povo, which is a 10-minute walk from Hotel Casas Novas.
